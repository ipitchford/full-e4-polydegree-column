## Method transfer

P7a provides a useful general recipe for effectivizing a qualitative simple
zero: normalize exact coefficients, identify the Fourier limit, retain a
first-order correction, bound a finite head and infinite tail, apply a
quantitative Newton theorem, and certify the compact remainder by intervals.
The newly explicit `R_X` line improves transferability because an adopter can
now trace the coarse high-degree bound directly to the source polydisc.

P7b packages a broadly reusable formal strategy.  Cross-multiplication avoids
field-only kernel reasoning; the one legitimate cancellation occurs in a
universal domain; and specialization recovers arbitrary commutative rings.
The theorem map, Apache-2.0 license, pinned project and zero-divisor test make
the development usable beyond this application.

The boundary article exposes a practical mathematical screening sequence:
given a candidate seed, first test the `kappa+1` pencil systems; only a passing
seed justifies the harder work of proving the `BN` geometric interface.  The
norm lemma does not grow with affine-line cover degree, so it remains usable
when the boundary component itself is not rational but has a finite
affine-line dicritical above it.

## Artifact experience

The three PDFs can be read independently.  Markdown, TeX and extracted text
support accessibility and reuse.  Reproduction commands are short and divided
by dependency environment.  The 375-file manifest verifies the exact review
object, while the full accepted parent prevents the six-sheet case from being
detached from its chart and boundary evidence.  The package avoids local
caches and experimental artifacts despite preserving both authoritative
finite ledgers.

   review by Mathlib contributors.
