## Editorial assessment

The package contains a coherent principal result and two legitimately useful
companions.  The principal paper proves the full `e=4` Polydegree containment
for every `d >= 2` (Theorem A, pp. 1--2 and proof on p. 12).  This is an
infinite completion of a published finite range, not a larger numerical table:
the paper supplies an exact scaling, a Fourier limiting branch, a rigorous
eventual threshold, and a finite interval bridge.  That is a potentially
leading result in the narrow specialist area of plane polynomial-automorphism
strata, assuming external experts validate the new proof and priority search.

The formal paper is correctly positioned as an assurance contribution.  Its
arbitrary-commutative-ring theorems are stated cleanly on p. 2; pp. 2--3
explain why the generic-field proportionality argument is replaced by a
cross-multiplied cofactor identity, universal-domain cancellation, and
specialization.  It does not claim novelty for the classical identity.

The boundary-norm article is suitable as a separate short paper or companion
note.  The cover-degree cancellation (p. 2), finite-pencil equivalence (p. 3),
conditional transfer theorem (p. 4), six-sheet application and sharp method
limitation (p. 5) form a complete and intelligible arc.  The dependence on the
seed-specific geometric package is stated repeatedly rather than concealed.

## Originality and significance calibration

The priority audit distinguishes the published Lewis--Perry--Straub range,
Perry's weaker intersection result, Furter's fixed-multidegree work, and the
Orevkov/Borisov global geometry.  Its recommendation—high and plausibly new
for P7a, implementation originality for P7b, moderate conditional significance
for boundary transfer—is proportionate to the evidence.  The prohibited
phrases include “first,” “world-leading,” “JC2 solution,” and “HC4 solution.”
This is the correct release boundary.

## Presentation and package quality

All three PDFs are professionally typeset, have clear abstracts and theorem
statements, and contain explicit trust/limitation sections.  The 14/5/6-page

## Observations (non-blocking, not defects)

1. Submit the three papers separately and cite the common artifact archive,
   rather than asking one journal to treat the package as a single article.
2. A specialist journal handling the P7a paper should be told at submission
   that the finite range is computer-assisted and should assign at least one
   referee comfortable with Arb/interval Newton methods.
3. Any public description may say “submission-ready candidate” and “plausibly
   highly original on the completed search,” but should retain the package's
   explicit external-review and priority qualifications.

mathematicians.  This decision is internal quality control, not journal
