## Decision rule

Each reviewer assigns issues as Critical, Major, Minor, or Observation and
returns Accept, Minor Revision, Major Revision, or Reject.  Any Critical,
Major, or Minor issue means `HOLD_FOR_REPAIR`; unanimous Accept with no such
issues is required.  A Critical Devil's Advocate finding forbids acceptance.
Reports are simulated independent internal AI roles, not external human peer
review.

## Independent roles

1. **Editor-in-Chief:** fit, coherent contribution, priority calibration,
   article separability, presentation and release boundary.
2. **Methodology and evidence reviewer:** interval proof architecture,
   coverage, receipts, deterministic replay, formal trust boundary and
   reproducibility.
3. **Domain mathematics reviewer:** plane polynomial automorphisms,
   Polydegree implication, resultant/cofactor signs, norm lemma, finite-pencil
   theorem and conditional geometry.
4. **Applications and cross-disciplinary reviewer:** reusability of the
   techniques, usability of artifacts, limitations and absence of inflated
   JC2/HC4 or practical claims.
5. **Devil's Advocate:** strongest plausible attack on theorem bridges,
   finite/infinite coverage, hidden domain assumptions, priority, companion
   dependence and proof-engine trust.

The reports are written independently and do not cite one another.  The final
synthesis may use only findings actually present in those reports.
