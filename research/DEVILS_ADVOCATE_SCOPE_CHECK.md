# Devil's Advocate checkpoint 1 — scope and answerability

**Decision:** `PASS` for investigation  
**Severity findings:** Critical 0; Major 0; Minor 0

## Challenges considered

### 1. Is the programme secretly dependent on solving an open conjecture?

No. JC2 and unrestricted HC4 are expressly outside the completion criteria.
P7a has three graded valid outcomes, and P4/P5 can terminate in a sharp
counterexample or no-go theorem. Completion nevertheless requires a real new
mathematical result and cannot be satisfied by process documentation alone.

### 2. Is “minimal-obstruction dossier” an escape hatch for abandoning P7a?

No. The dossier must be machine-checkable and prove every reduction up to the
first unresolved analytic bound or finite case. A narrative saying the problem
is difficult does not qualify.

### 3. Could a green Lean build falsely satisfy P7b?

The methodology blocks this. Completion requires forbidden-token scans,
`#print axioms`, source-to-statement comparison, boundary-degree coverage and a
separate specialization theorem. The discovered local skeleton is explicitly
recorded as unproved provenance, not as a base acceptance artifact.

### 4. Is P4/P5 generalization too vague to falsify?

The first output is an abstract norm lemma with enumerated hypotheses. Each
architecture test must either verify those hypotheses or exhibit the exact
one that fails. The gate requires a theorem or proved sharp limitation beyond
the parent.

### 5. Could literature search overstate novelty?

The search protocol includes adverse aliases and claim-level source inspection.
The highest internal label is `PLAUSIBLY_NEW`; “first” and “world-leading”
remain prohibited without external specialist priority review.

### 6. Is the scope too broad for coherent manuscripts?

The programme is a portfolio, not a single paper. P7a, P7b and P4/P5 will have
separate publication units and evidence ledgers. The successor release is the
container binding them to one parent and one integrity process.

## Conditions carried forward

1. Do not let work on the low-risk P7b formalization displace the required new
   mathematical result.
2. Do not infer full finite closure from asymptotic numerical experiments.
3. Search the exact hypotheses and proof mechanisms, not only manuscript titles.
4. Preserve the author-confirmation requirement for funding and competing
   interests before any external submission.
