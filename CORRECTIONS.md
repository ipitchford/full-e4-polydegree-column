# Corrections

## 2026-10-27 — errata to full-e4-polydegree-column 0.1.0-candidate (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22072044)

The archived PDF, the manuscript source, the Zenodo deposit and the tag `v0.1.0-candidate` are unchanged. These errata were confirmed by the Evidence Press archive audit and are also shown at <https://evidencepress.org/releases/full-e4-polydegree-column/>.

1. Corrected a description of the proof in §1, after (1.4). The archived text says the proof “is computer-assisted only on the finite interval 20 ≤ d < 20000”. The degrees d ≥ 20000 are covered by a machine-checked envelope certificate, as the abstract and §§4.8, 5 and 9 state. The sentence should read: “Its proof is computer-assisted throughout: interval-Newton certificates cover the finite interval 20 ≤ d < 20000, and an envelope certificate checked in exact rational and 256-bit Arb arithmetic covers all larger degrees.”

   This corrects how the method is described, not the result. No theorem, certificate, computation or stated result is affected.
