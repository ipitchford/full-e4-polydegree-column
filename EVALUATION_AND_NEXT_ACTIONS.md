# Evaluation and next actions

## What reaches the highest level

### 1. Full effective `e=4` Polydegree theorem

This is the strongest output.  It advances from the published finite range
`2<=d<20` to every `d>=2`.  Its significance is not the 14,985-case ledger
alone: the Fourier simple-zero analysis and rigorous eventual envelope turn an
infinite problem into a finite certificate.  The package contains exact
normalization checks, outward Arb intervals, normal/optimized replays, a
separate structural audit, analytic handoff and explicit trust boundary.

**Assessment:** formally presentable and potentially leading/world-class in
the narrow Polydegree area, but use “plausibly highly original and significant
on the documented search,” not “first” or “world-leading,” until external
specialists confirm the proof and priority.

### 2. Universal bordered-Jacobian Lean development

The underlying determinant/resultant idea is classical, but the end-to-end
arbitrary-ring formalization is substantial.  It removes field/rank shortcuts,
performs cancellation only in a universal integral domain, specializes to
arbitrary commutative rings, and tests a zero-divisor example.

**Assessment:** high-rigour, reusable formal-assurance contribution; best
released as a companion artifact/paper, not marketed as a new Jacobian result.

### 3. Boundary-norm finite-pencil transfer

The cover-degree-free exponent bound and finite-pencil equivalence are clean
and useful.  Their significance is conditional: each new seed still needs the
explicit `BN` geometric package.  The six-sheet application legitimately uses
the accepted companion chain, and `A=1,B=s` shows the algebraic method cannot
be weakened in the obvious way.

**Assessment:** publishable short theorem/companion note of moderate specialist
significance.  It should not be sold as new global Keller-map geometry.
